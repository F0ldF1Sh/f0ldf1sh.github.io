**Tsinghua University · May 2026–present**

This project investigates whether millimeter-wave radar can reconstruct continuous photoplethysmography (PPG) waveforms without a contact sensor, including when the radar recording shifts spatially.

I implemented an encoder designed to handle spatial shifts and led model training and downstream evaluation.
