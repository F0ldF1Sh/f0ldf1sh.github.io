**Tsinghua University · January 2026–present**

Electrocardiography (ECG), photoplethysmography (PPG), and blood pressure waveforms capture different aspects of cardiovascular activity. This project asks whether a single model can reconstruct missing segments or generate one waveform from other available signals.

I designed and trained a shared codec and a hierarchical autoregressive model for these tasks. I also worked on segmenting long waveforms into patches to make training and inference more efficient.
